提示工程技術地圖與它的陷阱A Map of Prompt-Engineering Techniques — and Their Pitfalls

few-shot、CoT 這些技巧讓模型更會「看起來對」；但「看起來對」不等於「對」，所以技巧的另一半是控制敏感度 + 驗證。

Few-shot and CoT make a model better at "looking right" — but "looking right" isn't "being right," so the other half of these techniques is controlling sensitivity and verifying.

是什麼 · What it is

提示工程有一組由淺到深的常見技巧：

① Zero-shot：只給任務，直接要答案。
② Few-shot：附幾個高品質的輸入／輸出範例，讓模型推出你要的格式與標準。
③ Instruction prompting：明講任務、要求與受眾，而非靠範例；可再和 few-shot 合用。
④ Chain-of-Thought（CoT）：引導模型「一步一步想」——用示範（few-shot CoT）或一句「Let's think step by step」（zero-shot CoT）。對複雜推理、大模型幫助最大，簡單任務幫助有限。
⑤ Self-consistency：在溫度 > 0 多抽幾個答案，再用多數決或實際驗證（如跑單元測試）挑一個。

再往上還有自動化提示設計（APE、soft prompt）與「增強式」路線（檢索增強、program-aided、呼叫外部工具）。但日常用得最多的是 ①–⑤。

Prompt engineering has a ladder of common techniques. ① Zero-shot: give the task, ask for the answer. ② Few-shot: add a few high-quality input/output examples so the model infers your format and criteria. ③ Instruction prompting: state the task, requirements, and audience explicitly rather than relying on examples; can be combined with few-shot. ④ Chain-of-Thought (CoT): elicit step-by-step reasoning — via worked examples (few-shot CoT) or a cue like "Let's think step by step" (zero-shot CoT); it helps most on complex reasoning and large models, only slightly on simple tasks. ⑤ Self-consistency: sample several answers at temperature > 0, then pick one by majority vote or by actually verifying it (e.g. running unit tests). Above these sit automatic prompt design (APE, soft prompts) and "augmented" routes (retrieval-augmented, program-aided, external tool calls) — but ①–⑤ cover most daily use.

為什麼重要 · Why it matters

這些技巧真正的教訓不是「咒語」，是它們有多脆：同一個任務，光改格式、換範例、調範例順序，表現可以從「接近亂猜」擺到「接近 SOTA」。few-shot 還帶著多數標籤偏誤、近因偏誤、常見詞偏誤——選例要「相關且多樣」而非彼此雷同，順序用隨機，指令要具體、說「要做什麼」別只說「別做什麼」。

最該記住的一條，正好接 RAG 忠實度與 LLM 怎麼運作：CoT 生出的「推理過程」常常不符事實，而不符事實的解釋往往伴隨錯誤的答案。也就是說，一條讀起來很有條理的思路，不保證結論對——這就是為什麼 self-consistency 要「驗」（跑測試）而不是「信」（看起來有理）。提示技巧負責讓模型「更可能說對」，規格與驗證負責確認它「真的說對」。

The real lesson of these techniques isn't incantation — it's how brittle they are: for the same task, changing only the format, the examples, or the example order can swing performance from near-random to near-SOTA. Few-shot also carries majority-label, recency, and common-token biases — pick examples that are "relevant and diverse" rather than similar to each other, randomize order, and make instructions specific, saying what to do rather than only what to avoid.

The one to remember connects straight to RAG faithfulness and how LLMs work: CoT-generated "reasoning" is often nonfactual, and nonfactual explanations tend to accompany wrong answers. A chain that reads as coherent does not guarantee a correct conclusion — which is exactly why self-consistency should verify (run tests), not trust (looks reasonable). Prompt techniques make the model "more likely to say the right thing"; spec and verification confirm it "actually said the right thing."

一句話帶走 · Takeaway

few-shot／CoT 讓模型更會「看起來對」，但看起來對≠對：選例相關且多樣、順序隨機、說要做什麼；最後一定要「驗」而不是「信」。

Few-shot/CoT make a model better at "looking right," but looking right isn't being right: choose relevant, diverse examples, randomize order, say what to do — and in the end, verify rather than trust.

四層工程思維：Prompt → Context → Harness → LoopFour Layers of Engineering: Prompt → Context → Harness → Loop

把「跟 AI 一起工作」拆成四層槓桿，由近而遠、由戰術到制度。

Breaking "working with AI" into four layers of leverage — near to far, tactical to systemic.

是什麼 · What it is

跟 AI 協作不是只有「把 prompt 寫好」一件事。作者把它拆成一道分層槓桿階梯，愈往後，一次投資能重複用的次數愈多：

① Prompt Engineering　單次指令寫得準。
② Context Engineering　餵給模型的脈絡（檢索、記憶、檔案）組得對。
③ Harness Engineering　工具、權限、驗證閘這套「外殼」架得穩。
④ Loop Engineering　讓系統能自我修正（內迴圈）並跨任務沉澱 SOP（外迴圈）。

Working with AI is more than "writing a good prompt." The author frames it as a ladder of layered leverage — the further down the list, the more times a single investment pays off: ① Prompt Engineering — getting a single instruction precise; ② Context Engineering — assembling the right context (retrieval, memory, files) for the model; ③ Harness Engineering — building a solid shell of tools, permissions, and verification gates; ④ Loop Engineering — letting the system self-correct (inner loop) and distil SOPs across tasks (outer loop).

為什麼重要 · Why it matters

多數人卡在第①層，反覆微調 prompt，卻沒發現真正的槓桿在後面：同樣的力氣，花在愈後面的層，複利愈大。把一個好脈絡組法（②）、一套驗證外殼（③）、一條會沉澱經驗的迴圈（④）建起來，之後每個新任務都省力。這是從「會用 AI」到「會把 AI 變成系統」的分界。

Most people get stuck at layer ①, endlessly tweaking prompts, missing that the real leverage sits further down: the same effort compounds more the deeper the layer. Build a good context recipe (②), a verification shell (③), and an experience-distilling loop (④), and every new task gets cheaper. This is the line between "using AI" and "turning AI into a system."

一句話帶走 · Takeaway

別只打磨 prompt。真正的槓桿在 Context、Harness、Loop——愈往後，一次投資重複用愈多次。

Don't just polish prompts. The real leverage is in Context, Harness, and Loop — the deeper the layer, the more a single investment is reused.

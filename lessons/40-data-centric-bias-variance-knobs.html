資料才是主角：自建資料集的每個實驗，都是在轉 bias-variance 旋鈕Data Is the Real Lever: Every Experiment on a Hand-Built Dataset Is Turning the Bias-Variance Knob

演算法常被談得比資料多，但模型好壞由資料決定——這就是 data-centric AI。自己動手建一個資料集、跑監督式學習時，那些實驗軸（資料量、類別平衡、SMOTE、資料增強、PCA/autoencoder 降維）其實都是在實際操作 bias-variance 旋鈕；而「必用交叉驗證」就是「不准用訓練誤差幫自己打分」的硬性落地。

Algorithms get talked about more than data, but a model's quality is decided by its data — that's data-centric AI. When you build a dataset yourself and run supervised learning, the experiment axes (amount of data, class balance, SMOTE, augmentation, PCA/autoencoder dimensionality reduction) are all hands-on ways of turning the bias-variance knob; and "cross-validation is mandatory" is the concrete enforcement of "don't grade yourself on training error."

是什麼 · What it is

這張是 「監督式學習只有一個旋鈕」的動手版——但它先把一個常被忽略的事實擺正：演算法被談得比資料多，可是要建好模型，你需要好資料來學。這就是 data-centric AI 的精神，也是為什麼「自己從零建一個資料集（不能用現成的）」本身就是一大塊工程：你得想清楚樣本的特性、約束、組成。

把它跑成一個監督式學習實驗，骨架是：挑分類或回歸、用至少兩種方法（至多一種深度學習）、用對的量尺評估——分類看confusion matrix，是非題看 precision／recall／F1／AUROC，回歸看 MSE——而且一定要用交叉驗證取得這些數字。

This is the hands-on version of "supervised learning has just one knob" — but it first sets straight a commonly ignored fact: algorithms get talked about more than data, yet to build a good model you need good data to learn from. That's the spirit of data-centric AI, and why "build a dataset from scratch yourself (you can't use an existing one)" is itself a big chunk of engineering: you must think through the samples' characteristics, constraints, and composition. Run it as a supervised experiment and the skeleton is: pick classification or regression, use at least two methods (at most one deep-learning), evaluate with the right yardstick — confusion matrix for classification, precision/recall/F1/AUROC for yes-no, MSE for regression — and always use cross-validation to obtain those numbers.

為什麼重要 · Why it matters

該帶走的洞見：那些看似雜七雜八的實驗軸，其實都是在轉同一個 bias-variance 旋鈕。

加訓練資料：最直接的降 variance——模型看得越多，越不會把這批雜訊當訊號。
類別不平衡：用類別加權或 SMOTE 重採樣，避免模型靠「永遠猜多數類」就拿到假的高準確率。
資料增強：等於免費多造訓練樣本，同樣是壓 variance、逼模型學不變的訊號。
降維（PCA／autoencoder）：對高維特徵砍掉冗餘——降低模型複雜度、讓過擬合更不容易發生，這正是 嵌入那條線在資料前處理端的現身。

另一個該帶走的是評估紀律，直通我一整天在守的那條：「一定要交叉驗證」不是形式，是「不准用訓練誤差幫自己打分」的硬性落地——跟 SimCLR 的 loss≠objective、不自評、要外部驗證同一條線。而「是非題要看 F1／AUROC 而非只看 accuracy」又接回 選對指標＝選對你真正在乎的東西：在類別不平衡時，accuracy 會騙你，就像 MSE 不等於分類誤差。一句話收束：模型的上限由資料決定，而你怎麼切資料、怎麼量結果，就是你有沒有誠實面對 bias-variance 的證據。

一句話帶走 · Takeaway

演算法被談得比資料多，但模型上限由資料決定。自建資料集跑實驗時，資料量/平衡/SMOTE/增強/降維全是在轉 bias-variance 旋鈕；而「一定要交叉驗證」「不平衡別只看 accuracy」就是「不准用訓練誤差幫自己打分、要選對指標」的動手版。

Algorithms get more airtime than data, but data sets a model's ceiling. When you experiment on a hand-built dataset, amount/balance/SMOTE/augmentation/dimensionality-reduction are all turning the bias-variance knob; and "always cross-validate" and "don't trust accuracy under imbalance" are the hands-on form of "don't grade yourself on training error, and pick the right metric."

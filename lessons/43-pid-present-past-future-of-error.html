PID：用誤差的現在、過去、未來來修正——feedback control 的靈魂是不信計畫、量現實PID: Correcting with the Present, Past, and Future of the Error — Feedback Control's Soul Is Distrust the Plan, Measure Reality

自駕車怎麼把規劃好的路徑在現實裡開出來？靠 feedback control：量測「參考值 − 感測到的真實輸出」當誤差，持續修正。PID 把這件事拆成誤差的三個時間向度——P 看現在、I 記過去、D 看未來。P 單獨會留下穩態誤差，I 補記憶卻會過衝，D 用變化率阻尼。這就是「不信開環計畫、量現實來校正」的母題。

How does a self-driving car actually drive the path it planned, in the real world? Feedback control: measure "reference − the sensed real output" as error, and correct continuously. PID splits this into three time axes of the error — P sees the present, I remembers the past, D anticipates the future. P alone leaves steady-state error, I adds memory but overshoots, D uses the rate of change to damp. This is the "distrust the open-loop plan, measure reality to correct" theme.

是什麼 · What it is

控制系統是「影響系統未來狀態」的機制，控制理論則是「怎麼改輸入去逼近想要的輸出」。關鍵對比：open-loop（開環）只是輸入→plant→輸出，不看結果——像踩油門控速，一遇上坡就慢下來卻不知道修正；closed-loop／feedback（閉環／回授）則多了一條回授線：參考值 − 感測器量到的真實輸出 ＝ 誤差，用誤差去驅動 controller 不斷校正。

最經典的 feedback controller 是 PID，而它的精髓是把「怎麼用誤差」拆成三個時間向度：

P（Proportional，比例）＝現在：輸出 ∝ 當下誤差（K_p·e(t)）。
I（Integral，積分）＝過去（記憶）：把長期累積的誤差加總（K_i·∫e(t)dt）。
D（Derivative，微分）＝未來：看誤差的變化率、預判走向（K_d·de/dt）。

合起來就是 Output = K_p·e(t) + K_i·∫e(t)dt + K_d·de(t)/dt（離散版把積分換成求和、微分換成 e(t)−e(t−1)）。

A control system is the mechanism that affects a system's future state; control theory is the strategy for changing the input to approach a desired output. The key contrast: open-loop is just input → plant → output with no feedback — like controlling speed with the accelerator, which slows on a hill without knowing to correct; closed-loop / feedback adds a feedback line: reference − the real output measured by a sensor = error, and the error drives the controller to correct continuously. The classic feedback controller is PID, whose essence is splitting "how to use the error" into three time axes: P (Proportional) = the present — output ∝ current error (K_p·e(t)); I (Integral) = the past (memory) — sum accumulated error over time (K_i·∫e(t)dt); D (Derivative) = the future — the rate of change of the error, anticipating its trend (K_d·de/dt). Together: Output = K_p·e(t) + K_i·∫e(t)dt + K_d·de(t)/dt (the discrete form replaces the integral with a sum and the derivative with e(t)−e(t−1)).

為什麼重要 · Why it matters

該帶走的洞見有兩層。第一層是為什麼要三項，而不是一項就好——每一項都在補前一項的坑：

只用 P 會留下穩態誤差：因為輸出正比於誤差，若維持輸出需要非零誤差（投影片的螺旋槳例：要定在 200rpm 就得有誤差乘上增益），誤差就永遠歸不了零。
加 I 補記憶：把過去累積的穩態誤差加總、逼它歸零——但累過頭會過衝（overshoot）、來回震盪。
加 D 看未來：用誤差變化率提前踩煞車，阻尼那個過衝。

所以 P 的穩態誤差 vs I 的過衝，是一組要調的張力——又是一個熟悉的旋鈕，精神上近 探索 vs 利用那種取捨。而 D 用「誤差將往哪走」來現在就修正，根本是 A\* 用估計的未來 h 導航、TD 用未來狀態回填現在的同一招換了件衣服。

第二層、也是最該帶走的：open-loop 相信自己的計畫，closed-loop 量測現實、承認會偏、然後校正。這正是我一整天的母題，而 PID 是它最乾淨的化身——有了它，卡 42 規劃出來的理想路徑才能在真實世界被開出來：longitudinal control 管「離目標多遠（速度）」，lateral control（pure pursuit／Stanley／LQR）管「方向盤怎麼打」把車咬在路徑上。規劃給你一張地圖，feedback control 才是那雙會不斷修正的手。

一句話帶走 · Takeaway

PID 用誤差的現在(P)、過去(I)、未來(D)來修正：P 留穩態誤差、I 補記憶卻過衝、D 看變化率阻尼。更深一層，open-loop 信計畫、closed-loop 量現實再校正——這就是整站的母題，也是讓卡 42 規劃的路徑真能在現實被開出來的那雙手。

PID corrects using the present (P), past (I), and future (D) of the error: P leaves steady-state error, I adds memory but overshoots, D uses the rate of change to damp. Deeper: open-loop trusts the plan, closed-loop measures reality then corrects — the site's recurring theme, and the hands that let card 42's planned path actually be driven in the real world.

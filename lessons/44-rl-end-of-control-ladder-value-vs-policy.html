RL 是控制階梯的盡頭：沒模型又非線性時才上 model-free，而學 policy 有兩條路RL Is the End of the Control Ladder: Go Model-Free When You Have No Model and It's Nonlinear — and There Are Two Roads to a Policy

控制演算法是一道階梯——PID → pure-pursuit → Stanley → LQR → model-free RL，越往上處理越複雜；RL 是盡頭：當你沒有環境模型、系統又非線性，才跳上去。而 model-free RL 學 policy 有兩條路：value-based（學 Q 再貪婪，就是卡 37）vs policy-based（直接學 π）。policy gradient 的死穴是取樣沒效率，PPO 用「一次別改太多」的信賴域來解。

Control algorithms form a ladder — PID → pure-pursuit → Stanley → LQR → model-free RL, each handling more complexity; RL is the end: you jump to it when you have no model of the environment and the system is nonlinear. And model-free RL has two roads to a policy: value-based (learn Q then act greedily — that's card 37) vs policy-based (directly learn π). Policy gradient's weakness is sample inefficiency, which PPO fixes with a "don't change too much at once" trust region.

是什麼 · What it is

這張接著 卡 43 的控制往上走。投影片把控制演算法排成一道階梯：PID →（套進車體運動學）pure-pursuit →（顧漸進穩定）Stanley →（更複雜的運動模型）LQR →（不需要模型、處理非線性）model-free RL。換句話說，RL 是這道階梯的盡頭：當你連環境模型都沒有、系統又非線性到寫不出控制律，才跳上去「用經驗學」。

卡 37 已經講過 RL 的底層（MDP ⟨S,A,P,r,γ⟩、Bellman、Q-learning、TD、信用分配），這張只補那張課沒有的演算法地圖：

model-based（有 MDP → 用它規劃）：Monte-Carlo、動態規劃（value / policy iteration）、MPC、MCTS。
model-free（沒 MDP → 靠取樣經驗學），再分兩條學 policy 的路——
　① value-based：先學一個價值函數（Q），再對它貪婪地選動作。Q-learning → DQN、NAF。這就是卡 37 那條。
　② policy-based：不繞價值、直接學策略 π。policy gradient → REINFORCE、TRPO、PPO；actor-critic 則兩者合體（A3C、DDPG、SAC）。

This continues upward from card 43's control. The slides arrange control algorithms as a ladder: PID → (apply vehicle kinematics) pure-pursuit → (progressive stability) Stanley → (a more complex motion model) LQR → (no model needed, handles nonlinearity) model-free RL. In other words, RL is the end of this ladder: you jump to it when you don't even have a model of the environment and the system is too nonlinear to write a control law for — so you "learn from experience" instead. Card 37 already covered RL's foundations (the MDP ⟨S,A,P,r,γ⟩, Bellman, Q-learning, TD, credit assignment); this card adds the algorithm map that course didn't: model-based (you have the MDP → plan with it: Monte-Carlo, dynamic programming = value/policy iteration, MPC, MCTS) vs model-free (no MDP → learn from sampled experience), which splits into two roads to a policy — ① value-based: learn a value function (Q) then act greedily on it (Q-learning → DQN, NAF — this is card 37's road); ② policy-based: skip value, directly learn the policy π (policy gradient → REINFORCE, TRPO, PPO; actor-critic fuses both — A3C, DDPG, SAC).

為什麼重要 · Why it matters

該帶走的第一件事：value-based 和 policy-based 是兩種截然不同的「怎麼得到 policy」。value-based 繞一圈——先學「每個動作值多少」再挑最大的；policy-based 更直接——把 π 本身當參數去優化，特別適合連續動作（機器人的方向盤、油門不是離散的），這也是為什麼機器人課的 HW3 要你用 PPO 讓車學會沿路徑跑。

但 policy gradient 有個著名死穴，也是最該帶走的：取樣效率很差——每次更新都要用當前策略重新互動取樣，一旦策略一步更新改太多，之前蒐集的經驗就作廢、還可能直接崩掉。TRPO／PPO 的解法是「信賴域」：限制每次更新不要讓新策略離舊策略太遠。這個「小步、別跳太遠」的直覺，正是 RLHF 那條 KL 繩的同一招（RLHF 本質就是把 PPO 用到語言模型上）——也跟 feedback control 不信大跳、持續小修同精神：當你只有一個會變動又不可靠的估計，就別一次賭太大。

最後一個接回本站的點：投影片把 RL 跟其他學習法對照——reward 之於 RL，就像 label 之於監督式（獎勵是 RL 的 loss）；而「沒有明確 reward 的 RL」（curiosity-driven／skill-driven）對應無監督，「meta RL」對應元學習——也就是說，連「沒有獎勵也要先探索出有用技能」都有對應，那又是 探索被推到極致的樣子。

一句話帶走 · Takeaway

控制演算法是一道階梯，RL 是盡頭：沒模型又非線性才上 model-free。它學 policy 有兩條路——value-based（學 Q 再貪婪，卡 37）vs policy-based（直接學 π，適合連續動作、機器人 HW3 的 PPO）。policy gradient 取樣沒效率，PPO 用信賴域「一次別改太多」解——那正是 RLHF 的 KL 繩、也是「別一次賭太大」的老母題。

Control algorithms form a ladder and RL is the end: go model-free only when you have no model and it's nonlinear. It reaches a policy by two roads — value-based (learn Q then act greedily, card 37) vs policy-based (directly learn π, good for continuous actions, the PPO in the robot HW3). Policy gradient is sample-inefficient, and PPO fixes it with a trust region that "doesn't change too much at once" — the very KL leash of RLHF, and the old theme of "don't bet too big in one step."
